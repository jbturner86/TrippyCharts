Desktop only

TrippyCharts is built for a desktop or laptop browser with a mouse. Phones and tablets will look wrong, miss the cursor toy, and make the game nearly unplayable. Use a computer.

How to play

Toys fall in the background. Move your mouse so the cursor (the squid) eats them; it grows, then blobs and resets. Toy Store: pick up to 5 falling toys. The Toys slider sets how many fall. The SFX and Cursor toggles are in the left menu. Each chart has a VOL checkbox to show volume bars.

Play Game starts game mode fullscreen: charts hide, a boss toy floats around. Suck toys and spit them into the boss. Each hit is 1 point. After the 3rd boss kill a second boss appears, then another after the 4th, and so on. You have one life bar. Getting touched costs 20%. When you die, enter a name for high scores. Esc leaves the game.
