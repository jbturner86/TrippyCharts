How to play

Toys fall in the background. Move your mouse so the cursor (the squid) eats them; it grows, then blobs and resets. Toy Store: pick up to 5 falling toys. Density slider sets how many fall. Cursor / SFX / Music toggles are top-left.

Hide Everything or page Full Screen starts game mode: charts hide, a boss toy floats around. Suck toys and spit them into the boss. Each hit is 1 point. After the 3rd boss kill a second boss appears, then another after the 4th, and so on. You have one life bar. Getting touched costs 20%. When you die, enter a name for high scores. Full Screen locks the pointer on the page. Hide Everything keeps running if the mouse leaves the window.
